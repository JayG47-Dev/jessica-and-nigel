Where to Stay and How to Get Here

Arrivals and Accommodation

Our ceremony and reception are both in Naxxar, in the north of Malta — a small island, so most areas are within a 20–40 minute drive.

Where to Stay

  • The happy couple will be staying at the Hilton in St Julian’s. We’ve arranged a special rate at the Hilton Malta for 10–17 May 2027 — Book at Our Rate →
  • Should guests wish to choose alternative accommodation, we recommend the surrounding area of St Julian’s. Renowned for its stunning coastline, lively atmosphere, and abundance of dining and entertainment options, it provides the ideal setting for a memorable stay in Malta. With a variety of hotels and apartments to suit all tastes, guests will also benefit from being just a short 10–12-minute journey from the wedding venue.
  • Alternative accommodation search: Booking.com · Airbnb · Trivago

If the rate doesn’t apply automatically, enter corporate code 35238 under Special Rates.

Getting Here

  • Fly into Malta International Airport (MLA) — approx. 15 minutes from the airport to St Julian’s, and 20 minutes to Naxxar.
  • Direct flights from Dublin take around 3h 45m, with a daily service to Malta.
  • Malta is part of the EU — check entry requirements for your nationality ahead of travel.
  • Malta drives on the left-hand side, same as Ireland — you can hire a car with ease at the airport, or book before you travel if you wish.
  • Uber and Bolt are both readily available for getting around the island, and there are also regular bus routes from St Julian’s to Valletta (the capital) and Naxxar.
