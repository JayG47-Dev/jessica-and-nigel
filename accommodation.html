Accommodation

A few suggestions for making the most of your stay in Malta for our wedding weekend.

Staying Near Naxxar

Our ceremony and reception both take place in Naxxar, in the north of Malta, so staying nearby — or anywhere with easy road access — will make the day simplest. Malta is a small island, so most areas are within a 20–40 minute drive of Naxxar.

Popular Areas to Consider

Near the Venue

  • Naxxar & surrounding villages — closest to the church and Palazzo Parisio
  • Mdina / Rabat — historic, roughly 15 minutes away
  • Mosta & Attard — central, quiet, close by

Coastal & Lively

  • Sliema & St Julian’s — seafront hotels, restaurants and nightlife
  • Valletta & Floriana — the capital, full of character
  • Mellieħa & St Paul’s Bay — quieter, beach-side, a little further north

Getting Between Your Hotel & the Venue

We recommend hiring a car for the flexibility, or booking taxis/rideshares (Bolt is widely used in Malta) in advance for the day itself.
