What to Wear

Garden formal, Malta-in-May style — here’s a guide for both the ceremony and the party.

Garden Formal

Our ceremony is in a church and our reception is in the gardens of Palazzo Parisio, so we’re asking for garden formal attire — think elegant and polished, but breathable and comfortable enough for a warm Mediterranean evening outdoors.

For Him

  • Lightweight suit or smart trousers with a blazer — linen or cotton blends breathe best in the Malta heat
  • Tie optional; an open collar is perfectly fine for the evening
  • Smart shoes that can handle grass and gravel underfoot
  • Please avoid all-white outfits

For Her

  • Midi or maxi cocktail dress in a light, flowing fabric
  • A wrap or light layer for the evening once the sun goes down
  • Block heels or wedges are kinder to grass than stilettos
  • Please avoid white or ivory, reserved for the bride

A Note on Malta Weather

Mid-May in Malta is typically warm and sunny, with daytime temperatures around 22–26°C (72–79°F), cooling slightly once the sun sets. Light fabrics are your friend, and we’d suggest bringing a layer for the evening celebrations.
