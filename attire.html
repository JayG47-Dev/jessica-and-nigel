Dress Code

Formal Attire

We ask guests to please wear formal attire for the day of the wedding — elegant but breathable for a warm evening outdoors. Expect 22–24°C by day, cooling to 16–18°C in the evening — sunshine is likely with little chance of rain, so bring a light layer for after dark.

For Him

  • Gentlemen are requested to wear a suit and tie.
  • Black tie is reserved for the wedding party.

For Her

  • Ladies are requested to wear formal dresses or elegant evening attire.
  • Shoulders must be covered for the church ceremony — a light shawl or scarf to be worn, please.
  • Please avoid very short skirts for the ceremony, in keeping with the church’s dress code.
