Getting There

Travel to Malta

Everything you need to know about getting to our island celebration.

Flying In

All flights arrive into Malta International Airport (MLA), just outside Valletta and around 25–35 minutes from Naxxar. Malta is well connected to major UK and European airports, with plenty of direct routes — it’s worth booking early as May is a popular time to visit.

Entry Requirements

Malta is part of the EU and the Schengen Area. UK and EU passport holders can generally visit visa-free for short stays — please double check the latest requirements for your own nationality well ahead of travel, and make sure your passport is valid for your trip.

Getting Around

Car Hire

Malta drives on the left. Hiring a car gives the most flexibility for exploring the island before and after the wedding.

Taxis & Rideshare

Bolt is widely used across Malta and is an easy way to get around without driving.

Make a Trip of It

Malta has so much to offer beyond the wedding day — the walled city of Mdina, the capital Valletta, the Blue Lagoon on Comino, and beautiful coastal walks. If you’re able to arrive a few days early or stay on afterwards, we’d love for you to explore the island.
